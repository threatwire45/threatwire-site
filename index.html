How it works

  1. You tell us what you run

    Your sector, your main systems and software, and the vendors you depend on. It takes about thirty minutes.

  2. We watch and assess

    We follow new attacks and vulnerabilities as they appear and judge each one against your list.

  3. You get what applies

    An urgent alert when something critical touches your systems, a short weekly summary, and a full report for major incidents. You can also ask us “are we affected by this?” at any time.

What we cover

Built for small and mid-size teams without a dedicated threat analyst, such as software companies, crypto and fintech firms, healthcare providers and financial services.

What we do

  • Track attacks and vulnerabilities reported publicly around the world
  • Assess whether each one affects your technology
  • Explain the risk in plain language with prioritized actions
  • Supply indicators ready to load into your security tools
  • Answer your questions about specific threats

What we don’t do

  • Monitor your network or devices
  • Replace incident response or round-the-clock security monitoring
  • Guarantee that an attack will be prevented
